Where we meet

So far: San Juan, Cartagena de Indias, Rio de Janeiro, Medellin, Nairobi, and Johannesburg. In 2027: Santiago de Cali in February, Accra in July, and Atlanta on a date to be announced.

Our founder

Cordell Carter founded Project on Belonging, Festival of the Diaspora, and Expectant Advisory. He curates more than three dozen off the record leadership seminars a year, reaching more than 1,000 leaders.

Bio and Speaking Requests

Why we started

Project on Belonging was launched in 2021 to create spaces for thoughtful, curious, globally minded leaders all over the Americas to build community, collaborate, and grow together.

What we mean by belonging

Belonging is civic, and it is economic. We connect the cultural identity of the diaspora to tangible economic development: partnerships, ventures, and investment.
