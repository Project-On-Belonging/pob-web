Diaspora Dialogues

Next edition: Accra, Ghana

Mid-July 2027. Past editions have convened leaders in Nairobi and Johannesburg, building a cross-border network that treats the continent and its diaspora as one conversation rather than two.

What happens in the room

Diaspora Dialogues is built for the leaders doing cross-border work every day: institutions investing on the continent, entrepreneurs building bridges, and funders looking for partners who already have the relationships in place. It is smaller than the Festival and built for depth over scale.
