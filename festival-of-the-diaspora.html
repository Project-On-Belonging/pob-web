Festival of the Diaspora, San Juan

Sixth Annual Festival: A Diasporic Experience in the Pacific

February 25 to 27, 2027, in Santiago de Cali, Colombia, with an optional excursion day on February 28. Cali is home to the largest Black middle class in Latin America, and the city is rebuilding. This is the moment for the diaspora to meet in the Pacific, form partnerships, and invest.
