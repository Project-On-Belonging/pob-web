Palante Labs

A residential exchange based in Cali, Colombia. Leaders and entrepreneurs who met at a convening live and work alongside each other, in both directions, so an introduction becomes a working relationship.

Host or Fund a Residency

Three ways we build the exchange

Bilateral Residencies

Entrepreneurs and leaders trade places, working inside each other's organizations and markets for a defined stretch of time.

Executives-in-Residence

Senior leaders embed with partner institutions in Cali, trading expertise for firsthand exposure to the Pacific region's businesses.

Petronio Alvarez Delegation

Our yearly delegation to the Petronio Alvarez Festival, the largest celebration of Afro-Colombian culture in the Pacific region.

Who this is built for

Entrepreneurs in the Pacific region

Gain direct access to partners, capital, and markets beyond Colombia.

Young leaders

Build a working relationship with the region, not just a memory of a conference.

Institutions and funders

Place people on the ground where the relationships you have funded actually operate.
