Sixth Region Summit

Our U.S. convening, held every two years in the American South, on regional investment, civic participation, and economic opportunity.

Get Notified
Leaders at the Concordia Summit

Why "Sixth Region"

The African Union calls the diaspora its sixth region. Sixth Region Summit is where that region meets at home, treating the American South as a region with its own history, institutions, and stake in the platform's work, not a side note to the international conversation.

Next edition: Atlanta, Georgia

2027, date to be announced. Expect the same trust-building mechanism as Festival of the Diaspora, scaled to a biennial rhythm rooted in one region and its institutions.
